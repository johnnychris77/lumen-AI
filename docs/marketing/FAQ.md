# Frequently Asked Questions

**What is LumenAI?**
An AI-powered inspection, evidence-governance, and decision-support platform for
medical instruments with internal channels and lumens.

**Does the AI make clinical decisions?**
No. Analysis is **assistive** — it suggests finding categories. A qualified person
owns every disposition. Uncertain or higher-risk findings route to human review.

**Is it FDA cleared, HIPAA compliant, or SOC 2 certified?**
No such certification is claimed. Compliance posture is established with your
organization and the appropriate authorities.

**Does it replace technicians, reviewers, or manufacturer IFUs?**
No. LumenAI supports those roles and does not replace them or the manufacturer's
instructions for use.

**Is the data on the website real?**
No. The public site and interactive demo use **synthetic demonstration data** only —
no PHI, no production data.

**How is evidence governed?**
Each inspection produces a record — image, metadata, rationale, timestamps —
appended to a hash-chained audit trail. Each stage adds to the record rather than
overwriting it.

**What's a "baseline"?**
An approved reference an inspection can be compared against. Baselines are governed
records; an inspection may have an approved, pending, or missing baseline.

**How do we try it?**
Request a demonstration or a pilot from the contact page.
