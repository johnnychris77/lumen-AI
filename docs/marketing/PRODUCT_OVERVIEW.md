# LumenAI — Product Overview

**LumenAI is an AI-powered inspection, evidence-governance, and decision-support
platform for medical instruments with internal channels and lumens.** It turns
borescope inspection into structured, comparable, reviewable evidence — with a
qualified human owning every decision.

## The problem
Internal-channel inspection evidence today is often loose images, spreadsheets,
and tribal knowledge. It is hard to compare against a baseline, hard to review,
and hard to audit.

## What LumenAI does
1. **Captures** the lumen image and attaches structured metadata.
2. **Assists** the technician with suggested finding categories (assistive only).
3. **Compares** against an approved baseline when one exists.
4. **Routes** uncertain or higher-risk findings to a qualified human reviewer.
5. **Records** governed evidence — image, metadata, rationale, timestamps — to a
   hash-chained audit trail.
6. **Reports** trends and audit-ready evidence.

## What LumenAI is NOT
- Not an autonomous clinical decision-maker.
- Not a replacement for trained technicians, clinicians, infection prevention,
  quality review, or manufacturer instructions for use (IFUs).
- Not a diagnostic medical device. No FDA clearance, HIPAA/SOC 2, or other
  certification is claimed.

## Positioning (one line)
"Governed inspection evidence, with human accountability at every decision."

*Public demonstrations use synthetic data only — no PHI.*
