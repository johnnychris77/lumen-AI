# Sales Playbook

## Buyer map
| Persona | Cares about | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Hospital CEO | Risk, defensibility | Governed, audit-ready evidence |
| SPD Director | Throughput + accountability | Volume + review routing + baseline coverage |
| SPD Technician | Speed, clarity | Assistive analysis; human decision |
| Quality Director | Audit readiness | Traceable evidence, not spreadsheets |
| Infection Prevention | Reviewable visibility | Structured evidence supports review |
| Vendor Rep | Instrument trends | Anonymized trend signals |
| Biomed | Lifecycle | Digital twin per instrument |
| Investor | Model + scope | Honest, demonstrated evidence platform |

## Discovery questions
- How is internal-channel inspection evidence captured and stored today?
- How do you compare an inspection to an approved baseline?
- How would you assemble evidence for an audit or an incident review?
- Who reviews uncertain findings, and how is that recorded?

## Objection handling
- **"Is this a diagnostic/AI decision tool?"** No — assistive; a qualified person
  decides. No autonomous clinical decisions, no diagnostic-device claim.
- **"Is it FDA cleared / HIPAA / SOC 2?"** No certification is claimed; compliance
  posture is established with your organization and the appropriate authorities.
- **"What are the results/ROI numbers?"** The public demo uses synthetic data; we
  do not publish outcome, accuracy, or savings figures — a pilot measures value in
  your environment.
