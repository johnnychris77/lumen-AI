# LumenAI Explainer — Final Narration Script

**Runtime:** ~110 seconds. **Voice:** warm, measured, credible — a clinical
peer, not an announcer. **Read rate:** ~150 wpm; leave a beat between scenes.

The on-screen text and animation for each scene live in `VIDEO_STORYBOARD.md`;
the caption file is `frontend/public/site/lumenai-explainer.vtt`.

---

**[00:00–00:12] Scene 1 — The hidden problem**
> Some of the most important surfaces on a surgical instrument are also the
> hardest to see.

**[00:12–00:24] Scene 2 — Inspection limits**
> Today, borescope evidence can be hard to standardize, compare, review, and
> trace over time.

**[00:24–00:36] Scene 3 — Introduce LumenAI**
> LumenAI turns internal instrument inspection into structured, reviewable
> evidence.

**[00:36–00:48] Scene 4 — Capture**
> Identify the instrument. Capture the lumen image. Attach the context that
> makes it evidence.

**[00:48–01:00] Scene 5 — Analyze**
> Assistive analysis highlights areas for a reviewer — it never decides alone.

**[01:00–01:11] Scene 6 — Compare**
> When an approved baseline exists, the current inspection sits right beside it.

**[01:11–01:22] Scene 7 — Review**
> Uncertain or higher-risk findings route to a qualified reviewer.

**[01:22–01:34] Scene 8 — Govern**
> Every decision is recorded — with rationale, timestamps, permissions, and an
> audit trail.

**[01:34–01:44] Scene 9 — Learn**
> Over time, findings become trends across instruments, vendors, sites, and
> time.

**[01:44–01:50] Scene 10 — Close**
> LumenAI. Better visibility. Better evidence. Better decisions.

---

## Compliance notes for the read

- Never say "detects", "diagnoses", or any accuracy figure. "Highlights",
  "suggests", "for a reviewer" only.
- Keep the human-in-the-loop line (Scene 5, 7) — it is load-bearing.
- No claims about infection rates, FDA, HIPAA, compliance, or savings.
- If a lower-third disclaimer is added, use: "Demonstration. Decision support
  with human review — not autonomous diagnosis."
