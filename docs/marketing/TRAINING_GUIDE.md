# Training Guide

Outline for a future Training Center (`/training`, roadmapped). Role-based paths;
all materials use synthetic data.

- **Administrator:** tenant setup, roles/permissions, baselines, report templates.
- **Technician:** capture, metadata, reading assistive suggestions, documentation.
- **Reviewer / Quality:** review routing, dispositions, evidence, audit readiness.
- **Leadership:** dashboards, cross-site governance, reporting.
- **Library:** explainer video (real-video slot wired via `VITE_EXPLAINER_VIDEO_URL`), docs, FAQ.

Guardrail: training reinforces assistive-not-autonomous and human-final messaging.
