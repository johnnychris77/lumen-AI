# LumenAI Explainer — Voiceover Script (v2)

**Working title:** *From What We See to What We Know*
**Runtime:** ~116 seconds (100–120s target) at 30fps
**Audiences (simultaneous):** Sterile Processing professionals · Healthcare leadership · Quality/Infection Prevention · Medical device manufacturers · Strategic partners · Investors

**Core message:** LumenAI transforms internal instrument inspection from an
isolated visual observation into structured, governed, longitudinal inspection
intelligence — while keeping qualified professionals in control. **The
technician is the hero; the supervisor provides human oversight; LumenAI
connects the workflow.**

> The narration below is the source of truth for the burned-in captions and the
> sidecar files (`public/captions/lumenai-explainer.srt` / `.vtt`). Timecodes are
> the scene windows from `src/timing.ts`.

## Read direction

Calm, credible, unhurried. The person leads. Pause a beat before **"AI assists.
People decide."** (music dips under it) and again before the closing triad. No
hype, no fear, no financial promises.
